# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Strategy professionals, executives, architects and product leaders who need to understand Enterprise AI well enough to brief boards, challenge vendors, and make build-vs-buy calls — without becoming engineers. They arrive in three modes: a 5-minute pre-meeting skim, a 30-minute orientation, or a multi-week structured learning journey. They are fluent in tools like Stripe docs, Linear and Notion and will judge the product by that bar.

## Product Purpose

Enterprise AI Framework transforms a 16-document research package into an interactive learning platform. It answers: "How do all the pieces of Enterprise AI fit together, and how do I use that understanding to make better decisions?" Success = a learner moves from recognising terminology → understanding concepts → navigating the architecture → evaluating trade-offs → making informed decisions. The source documents remain the single source of truth; the site adds structure, connection, interaction and practice — never new facts.

## Brand Personality

Strategic, precise, calm. The voice of a trusted senior advisor: confident sentences, numbers over adjectives, no hype. Feels like Stripe documentation × Linear × an excellent interactive course. Warm crimson accent as the single confident thread through an otherwise ink-on-white cartographic system — a strategy atlas, not a tech dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard layouts; walls of identical cards
- Walls of text; Markdown files rendered in a browser
- Gradient-heavy "AI product" aesthetics (purple/blue glows, sparkle icons)
- Random animations and decorative visuals with no learning purpose
- Corporate LMS blandness (progress rings everywhere, gamification confetti)

## Design Principles

1. **The map is the interface.** Navigation is built around how concepts connect, not file order. Every screen should orient the learner on the seven-layer map.
2. **Understanding over information.** Every visual answers a learning question; every interaction tests or builds a relationship between concepts.
3. **Progressive disclosure.** Big picture first; drill-down on demand. Never force every learner down the same path.
4. **Aggressive hierarchy.** The most important idea on any screen is unmistakable. Whitespace and typography carry structure.
5. **Traceable honesty.** Source-backed guidance is visibly distinct from interactive navigation logic; uncertainty flagged in sources stays flagged.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, full keyboard operability for all interactive tools (map, quizzes, decision explorers), visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation, semantic landmarks and headings. Interactive visualisations always have a text-equivalent path.
